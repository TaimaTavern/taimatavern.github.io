Pass or Blast chat rules

Version 1, November 25, 2026. Applies to chat and nicknames in the Android game "Pass or Blast" (com.taimatavern.passorblast) by Taima Tavern.

The game asks you to accept these rules before your first chat message and before you change your nickname. Reading chat needs no acceptance.

  • Insults and threats are prohibited.
  • Harassment over nationality, gender, faith or other traits is prohibited.
  • Spam, ads and links are prohibited.
  • Posting personal data, yours or others', is prohibited: addresses, phone numbers, photos.
  • Sexual topics are prohibited. Player age: 13 and up.

What happens if you break them

Reports are reviewed by a moderator. A violation disables chat; a serious violation blocks the account.

If you see something bad

To report or block a player, tap the nickname in chat. The menu has Report and Block. A report sends us that player's latest messages so we can look at them. How we handle reports and how long we keep them is in the Privacy Policy.
