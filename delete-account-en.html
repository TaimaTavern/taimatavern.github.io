Русский Privacy Policy

Delete your Pass or Blast account

The game "Pass or Blast" (com.taimatavern.passorblast) by Taima Tavern.

Requesting deletion without the game. Email taimatavern@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pass or Blast account" and send your account number (in the game: profile, the line "Account no. ..."). If you don't have it, send your nickname and roughly when you played, and we'll find the account ourselves. We reply and delete within 30 days, usually in a few days.

How to delete in the game

Open your profile (the nickname plate in the lobby), tap "Delete account" at the bottom and confirm twice. The account and its data are erased right away. You need an internet connection and no match may be running.

What is deleted

  • The account and its number.
  • Progress: level, league, caps, keys, stashes, heroes, skins, stats.
  • The nickname.
  • Friends, friend requests, the blocked list, recent opponents.
  • The Google Play Games link (the Google player ID we received at sign-in).
  • Reports this account filed, and reports about it.
  • The device token.
  • The game's own statistics under this number (logins, matches, tutorial, spending, phone model and country): erased immediately, never part of the backups.
  • Chat and party messages: they live only in server memory and disappear on deletion.

What remains and for how long

  • Database backups on the server. They are made automatically every 6 hours and the latest 7 are kept. A deleted account drops out of them on its own, within 42 hours after deletion at the latest. We don't open them and don't use them to restore deleted accounts.
  • The server technical log: event times, account number and connection IP address, without nickname or email. There is no fixed retention period: old entries are removed automatically as the log fills up.
  • One irreversible hash of a Google ID (with a secret salt) and a time, if that Google account already received the invite-a-friend bonus. It stops deletion plus a new account from unlocking the bonus twice. The hash reveals no ID, account or nickname and isn't tied to the deleted account. Kept as long as the game runs.
  • Data the Yandex ads SDK and AppMetrica collect on their own (the device advertising ID, diagnostics). We don't store it; you can request it from Yandex. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

We don't collect the name or email of your Google account, contacts or precise location.

A deleted account can't be restored. If you launch the game again after deleting, it starts from zero with a new number.
